🏅 Champions Cup

Who: Top 8 per zone → 32 teams. Group stage → knockout → Final.

Prize: Trophy, cash prize (source: sponsorship pool), automatic qualification next season.

🛡️ Warriors Cup

Who: 4 zonal FA Cup winners + clubs ranked 9th–16th. 32 teams.

Reward: Winner promoted to Champions Cup (replaces bottom club if rules allow).
