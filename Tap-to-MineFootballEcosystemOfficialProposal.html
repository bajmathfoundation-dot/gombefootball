📄 Letter of Concern

A Call to Action for Local Football

September 8, 2026
To: Football Club Owners, League Organizers, and Community Stakeholders

Dear Football Community,

Local football is the heartbeat of communities worldwide. It nurtures talent, builds character, and unites people across all divides. Yet, despite its profound social impact, local football clubs are facing an existential crisis — dwindling funding, declining fan engagement, and an inability to compete with the financial might of professional leagues.

We've watched as passionate clubs, rich in history and community spirit, struggle to keep their lights on. Over 60% of local clubs operate at a loss, relying on the generosity of a few dedicated individuals rather than sustainable revenue models. Meanwhile, the modern fan — especially the younger generation — is more engaged with screens than stadiums, more active on social media than on the terraces.

This is not a problem that will solve itself. The current trajectory leads to a future where local football becomes a memory, replaced by the sterile, commercialized products of global entertainment conglomerates.

But there is hope. We believe the solution lies in the very thing that seems to be the problem: technology. The same screens that distract fans can be used to reconnect them. The same social media that captures attention can be channeled into revenue. The same gaming culture that keeps youth indoors can be redirected to support their local teams.

The Tap-to-Mine Football Ecosystem is our answer to this crisis. It's not just a game — it's a movement. A way for fans to actively participate in the financial success of their clubs. A way for teams to generate sustainable, ad-backed revenue. A way for the community to own its football future.

We're not asking for charity. We're offering a partnership — a proven, scalable, and self-sustaining economic model that benefits everyone: fans, teams, sponsors, and the community at large.

The time to act is now. The football we love depends on it.

— Engr. Saidu Ibrahim Jalo
CEO, Bajmath Support Programes & Foundation
Former Football Coach

🛡️ Economy Protection Mechanisms

🔒 Coin Backing

Every coin in circulation is backed by real ad revenue. The total coin supply can never exceed the reserve capital.

🔥 Deflationary Mechanics

Upgrades and in-game spending burn coins, reducing supply and maintaining value over time.

📊 Dynamic Minting

New coins are only minted when the backing ratio is >95%, ensuring the economy stays stable.

🔄 Redistribution

Coins flow through the economy: earn → spend → burn → new minting, creating a sustainable cycle.
