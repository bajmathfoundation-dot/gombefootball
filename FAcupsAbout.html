Competition Overview

The FA Cups competition features four distinct tournaments: Dam Cup, Hills Cup, Farmers Cup, and Cattle Cup. Clubs from both Tier 1 and Tier 2 participate in zonal competitions with a structured knockout format leading to the final matches.

Key Tournament Structure: The competition begins with zonal matches, followed by a knockout phase where Tier 1 and Tier 2 clubs compete in single matches until the final stages.

Competition Format

Tier Structure

  • All Tier 1 clubs enter the competition at Round Two
  • Tier 2 clubs are divided based on their performance:
    • Top 10 teams enter at Round Two
    • Bottom 10 teams start at Round One

Tournament Stages

Round One

10 Teams
  • Exclusively for the bottom 10 Tier 2 teams
  • Single-match knockout format
  • 8 teams eliminated based on table results
  • 2 teams advance to Round Two

Round Two

32 Teams
  • Participants: All Tier 1 clubs + Top 10 Tier 2 + 2 qualifiers from Round One
  • Single-match knockout format
  • 16 winners advance to the next stage

Round Three

16 Teams
  • Single-match knockout format
  • 8 winners advance to quarter-finals

Quarter-finals

8 Teams
  • Single-match knockout format
  • 4 winners advance to semi-finals

Semi-finals

4 Teams
  • Home and away (two-leg) format
  • 2 winners advance to the final

Final

2 Teams
  • Single match to determine the champion
  • Played at a neutral venue

Important Notes:

  • Tier 1 clubs enter directly at Round Two
  • Tier 2 top 10 enter at Round Two
  • Tier 2 bottom 10 start at Round One
  • Only semi-finals use home-and-away format
  • All other rounds are single-match knockouts

Key Tournament Rules

  • All matches except semi-finals are single-game knockout
  • Semi-finals are played over two legs (home and away)
  • Final is a single match at a neutral venue
  • In knockout stages, if scores are level after regulation time:
    • Extra time (two 15-minute halves) is played
    • If still tied, penalty shootout determines winner
  • In semi-finals, aggregate score determines winner
  • Away goals rule applies in semi-finals
