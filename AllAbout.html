Football Zones of Gombe

Northern Zone

Sahel League • Cattle Cup

LGAs: Dukku, Funakaye, Nafada

Cultural Traits: Pastoralist communities, Sahel plains, Fulani cultural heritage.

Football Identity: Teams reflect pastoral traditions with strong community connections.

Southern Zone

Hills League • Hills Cup

LGAs: Billiri, Kaltungo, Shongom

Cultural Traits: Kaltungo Hills, Tangale culture, fertile valleys.

Football Identity: Technical style influenced by hilly terrain, deep cultural connections.

Central Zone

City League • Dam Cup

LGAs: Gombe, Kwami, Akko

Cultural Traits: Urban hub around Gombe City, Gongola River, commerce.

Football Identity: Modern facilities, diverse population, corporate sponsorships.

Eastern Zone

Lands League • Farmers Cup

LGAs: Yamaltu/Deba, Balanga

Cultural Traits: Farmlands, Dadin Kowa Dam, agrarian economy.

Football Identity: Community-based clubs tied to farming cooperatives, seasonal scheduling.
