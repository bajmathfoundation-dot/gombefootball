About the Zone

The Northern Zone is home to pastoralist communities, characterized by its vast savannah landscapes and traditional livestock-rearing culture. This region experiences distinct wet and dry seasons that shape both the lifestyle and sporting calendar.

Football clubs in this zone often represent communities with deep-rooted pastoral traditions, and matches frequently coincide with cultural festivals and livestock markets.

Hills League

About the Zone

This region is the cultural heartland of the Tangale people, known for its picturesque hills and valleys. The terrain has influenced a distinct style of football with an emphasis on technical skills and agility.

Football clubs here are deeply connected to local cultural traditions, with many teams originating from community youth development programs that celebrate Tangale heritage through sport.

About the Zone

Centered around Gombe City, this urban hub features modern infrastructure and a diverse population. The City League represents the fastest-growing football region with state-of-the-art facilities.

Clubs in this zone benefit from corporate sponsorship and have developed sophisticated youth academies. The cosmopolitan nature of the city has created a dynamic football culture that blends traditional support with modern fan engagement.

Eastern Zone
Lands League

About the Zone

Located near the Dadin Kowa Dam, this agricultural region features fertile plains that support extensive farming. Football here follows the agricultural calendar with match schedules designed around planting and harvest seasons.

Clubs are often community-based with strong ties to local farming cooperatives. The Dam provides irrigation that maintains excellent pitch conditions year-round, making this zone known for its quality playing surfaces.

Relegation System

How clubs move between league tiers

Promotion & Relegation

The Gombe football pyramid features a structured relegation system to maintain competition quality across tiers:

  • Each Zonal Premier League (Tier 1) has 20 clubs
  • At the end of each season, the bottom 3 clubs are relegated
  • Relegated clubs move to their respective Zonal Championship (Tier 2)
  • Top 3 clubs from each Zonal Championship (Tier 2) are promoted to Premier League
  • This system ensures competitive balance across both tiers

Key Regulations

  • Relegation determined by final league position
  • In case of tie, head-to-head record then goal difference
  • Financial compliance required for promotion
  • Stadium standards must be met for Tier 1 participation
