one match · one shield · one family

symbol of togetherness

The Gombe Unity Shield is contested as a single, prestigious match between the reigning Champions Cup winner and the Warriors Cup holder. It embodies the harmony and mutual respect between the two pathways — elite and challengers — celebrating the football ecosystem of Gombe.

season curtain-raiser

Played on the weekend preceding the start of the new league season, the Unity Shield opens the football year with a festival of colors, uniting fans from all four zones. It is a non-eliminative honor cup — a pure exhibition of pride and unity.

Warriors Cup
The Unity Shield is not about revenge or ranking — it's a handshake in the form of football. The two champions wear a special edition sash, and the winner holds the shield for one year.

roots of unity

Introduced to honour the spirit of the two cups, the shield match encourages fans to celebrate both competitions. The trophy is crafted by local artisans from the four zones.

perpetual honor

If the same club holds both titles (a double), they are automatically awarded the Unity Shield. Otherwise, a one‑off gala match is scheduled at a neutral venue.
