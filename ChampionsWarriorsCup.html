Gombe Champions Cup

Premier competition for top-performing clubs

Qualification

  • Top 8 clubs from each zonal Premier League
  • Total of 32 qualifying teams (4 zones × 8 teams)

Competition Format

  • Group Stage:
    • 32 teams divided into 8 groups of 4 teams each
    • One club per zone in each group
    • Round-robin format with home and away matches
  • Knockout Stage:
    • Round of 16: Top 2 teams from each group advance
    • Quarterfinals: 8 teams compete
    • Semifinals: 4 remaining teams
    • Final: Single match to determine champion

Champions Prize

Prestigious trophy, prize money, and Automatic qualification

Qualification Rules

  • Only top 8 teams automatically qualify for next year's Champions Cup
  • If the winner finishes outside their zonal Top 8:
    • They replace the 8th-placed team in their zone

Gombe Warriors Cup

Competition for FA Cup winners and mid-table clubs

Qualification

  • Winners of each zonal FA Cup:
    • Cattle Cup, Hills Cup, Dam Cup, Farmers Cup
  • Clubs ranked 9th–16th in each zonal Premier League
  • Total of 32 teams (4 FA Cup winners + 28 league qualifiers)

Competition Format

  • Group Stage:
    • 32 teams divided into 8 groups of 4 teams each
    • One club per zone in each group
    • Round-robin format with home and away matches
  • Knockout Stage:
    • Round of 16: Top 2 teams from each group advance
    • Quarterfinals: 8 teams compete
    • Semifinals: 4 remaining teams
    • Final: Single match to determine champion

Warriors Prize

Winner qualifies for the following season's Gombe Champions Cup

Qualification Rules

  • Warriors Cup winner automatically qualifies for next year's Champions Cup
  • If the winner finishes outside their zonal Top 8:
    • They replace the 8th-placed team in their zone
  • Warriors Cup winner replaces the 16th-placed team if outside middle 8 (9th-16th)
